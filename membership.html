Membership

Home / Membership

Take your place in the global forum.

Whether you are called in London, admitted in New York, enrolled in Karachi, licensed in Dubai or qualified in Brussels, there is a seat for you at GLF.

What Membership Delivers

The standing of an institution, the reach of a network.

Practical benefits for the working lawyer, and a platform that carries the profession's voice further than any single firm can.

Global Referral Network

Verified counsel in eight jurisdictions, so you can refer or receive instructions with confidence anywhere the Forum reaches.

Accredited Education

International seminars, CPD-eligible training and joint scholarship across common-law and civil-law systems.

Recognition & Standing

Membership certification, delegate invitation letters and the credibility of a chartered international body.

Advocacy Platform

A collective voice on judicial independence, access to justice and the pressures facing lawyers worldwide.

Summits & Convenings

An annual Global Legal Summit and regional roundtables that put you in the room with peers and the bench.

Pro Bono & Impact

Coordinated pro bono initiatives that widen access to justice and give members meaningful ways to serve.
