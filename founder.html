The Founding President

“The law does not stop at a border, and neither should the lawyers who serve it. GLF was founded so that principle could have an institution behind it.”
Barrister Muhammad Sohail Anjum, Founder & First President

As Founder and First President, Barrister Muhammad Sohail Anjum brought together practitioners from many legal traditions under a single charter. His vision for GLF is a forum that is genuinely global in reach yet uncompromising in standard. It is meant to be a professional home for lawyers wherever their work takes them, and a credible voice for the profession in the forums that shape it.
